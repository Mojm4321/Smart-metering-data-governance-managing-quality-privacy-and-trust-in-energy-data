# Smart Metering Data Governance: Managing Quality, Privacy And Trust In Energy Data

# Introduction & Context
Smart meters are digital devices that record household eletricity consumption and transmit usage data at regular intervals. This enables more accurate billing, improved visibility of energy use and greater opreational efficiency across the energy system. In the UK, the smart meter programme is supplier-led, supported by government policy (originally overseen by BEIS) and regulated by Ofgem. The programme aims to modernise hte national energy infastructure, reduce inefficiences and support more cost-effective energy use for households over the long term.
